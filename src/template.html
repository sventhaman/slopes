R rewinds 3 seconds, as often as you like. C switches camera, M opens the mountain view, Esc pauses. Land with your skis lined up with your direction of travel; switch landings count. A gamepad works too: left stick, A to jump, X to grab, Y for the wingsuit, B to rewind.
